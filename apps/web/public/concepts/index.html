Woodbrook Community Hub · design exploration

Six ways a neighbourhood can feel like home.

Each standalone concept uses the same resident-first purpose. Concept 02 is the current favourite; concepts 04–06 explore three more community-hub directions around gathering, belonging, and shared contribution. Transport is not part of their primary content.
