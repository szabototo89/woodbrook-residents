What happens at Spotless

One visit.
A complete routine.

Stephanie assesses your dog on arrival and agrees a suitable trim for their breed, coat condition and lifestyle.

01

Assess & prepare

Coat check, careful brushing and any required dematting.

Every groom starts with the dog in front of us.
02

Wash & dry

Two warm shampoos, followed by thorough coat drying.

Clean coat, ready for a precise finish.
03

Trim & style

Breed-standard grooming or a practical style you agree.

Designed to suit both the dog and daily life.
04

Finish & check

Nails, ears and skin are checked as part of the routine.

We’ll flag anything that may need veterinary attention.

Coat condition can change the time and work involved. Final scope and price should be agreed directly with Stephanie.

The person behind the groom

Practised hands.
A clear eye.

Stephanie Lawless has groomed professionally since 2003. She is City & Guilds qualified, regularly attends grooming seminars and has placed in Irish Dog Grooming Championship competitions, including Reserve Groomer of the Year in 2010.

Before your appointment

Three small things make the visit easier.

  1. Take a good walk

    A little exercise and a toilet break can help your dog settle.

  2. Use a secure collar & lead

    Keep your dog safely attached when arriving and leaving.

  3. Book ahead

    Appointments are limited, so advance notice is recommended.

Cancellations

Cancellations are free of charge. Missed appointments are charged at 50% of the visit cost.
