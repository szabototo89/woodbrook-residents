Good to know

Before their visit.

Simple answers to help the appointment feel easy for you and your dog.

How should I prepare my dog?+

Exercise your dog where possible and give them time to toilet before arriving. Please use a secure lead and collar.

When should I collect my dog?+

We’ll call as soon as your dog is ready. Please wait until you hear from us before arriving—seeing you early can make it difficult to safely finish their trim.

How is the final price decided?+

It depends on breed, size, coat condition, required styling and any additional handling or dematting time. We’ll discuss it with you.

Can you groom a nervous or reactive dog?+

Tell us what your dog needs when requesting an appointment. Some dogs need extra time and handling; we’ll discuss what is safe and comfortable.

How do I care for their coat between grooms?+

Regular brushing and combing helps prevent matting. Your groomer can recommend the right tools and frequency for your dog’s coat.
