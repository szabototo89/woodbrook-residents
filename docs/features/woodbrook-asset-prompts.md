## Logo symbol — `woodbrook-logo.png`

Refine this Woodbrook Residents logo into pristine flat graphic artwork. Keep the
recognisable three ivory house rooflines, simplified flowing ivory shoreline and
small golden sun. Correct all mottled areas and rendering imperfections. Make the
entire canvas a single SOLID opaque forest-green #254f40 colour, edge to edge, not
rounded, no margin, no transparent pixels. Place the balanced house/shoreline/sun
emblem centrally inside this square with 15% green inner padding. All ivory shapes
must be SOLID flat #f8f7f2, sun SOLID flat #f5dc82. No colour variations, gradients,
shading, texture, shadows, dark smudges, glow, shine, speckles, words or watermark.
This is the final small-size production website logo, not a photo or mockup. Use
the fewest clear shapes, keep the wave to two elegant strokes, strong silhouette.

## Header wordmark — `woodbrook-wordmark.png`

Edit this brand lockup. REPLACE ALL serif typography with a refined friendly
HUMANIST SANS-SERIF wordmark. No serifs anywhere. Exact wording: "Woodbrook
Residents", one line, mixed title case. Medium-light weight around 450, open
counters, gentle rounded terminals and natural humanist proportions, clean
contemporary letter spacing, inspired by readability and warmth of Frutiger or
Avenir, not a geometric tech startup font. The type must be visibly quieter,
lighter and smaller than the previous serif. Preserve the small
homes/shoreline/golden sun symbol on the left; its final usage is 32px tall and the
type equivalent to 17px. 10px visual gap. NO subtitle or second line. Keep only the
symbol and exact name. Deep forest-green type on completely flat pure white
background. Crisp opaque clean artwork, no texture, glow, shadow or gradient.
Frame the artwork TIGHTLY edge to edge with only 2% white outer padding, NO large
blank top/bottom or left/right margins. Wide horizontal 5:1 lockup, not a
presentation board. No W monogram. Output only the finished compact website logo.

## Coastal annotation — `coast-note.png`

Create a flat production website decoration image, on a PURE WHITE #ffffff
background. The graphic is ONLY a friendly elegant handwritten dark blue-green ink
note, exact words on two lines: "Our community" on line one, "by the coast" on line
two. Below the words is one thin curved handwritten arrow sweeping downward then
to the right. Natural legible restrained pen lettering, like a personal note. Dark
solid opaque ink #315566, clean sharp antialiasing. Gently rising baseline, compact
balanced composition with 5% white margin. Absolutely no glow, mist, transparency,
shading, smudges, grey areas, texture, paper effect, shadows, buildings, photograph,
embellishments or watermark. The entire background is perfectly solid pure white;
the strokes alone are dark blue-green. This white-background bitmap will be blended
over a coastal hero photograph.
