# Woodbrook Residents brand assets

Status: Available

## Job to be done

When residents arrive at the site, I want a clear, welcoming coastal identity
that remains recognizable on a phone, computer or browser tab.

## Visible behavior

- A compact generated sans-serif header wordmark pairs Woodbrook Residents
  with a neighbourhood symbol inspired by homes, shoreline and sun. It has no
  subtitle and leaves room for navigation.
- The symbol is a standalone image logo and browser identity asset; no W
  monogram is required.
- A handwritten coastal note and curved arrow are one image asset,
  rather than live decorative text in the hero.
- The brand home link retains its accessible name. Decorative images have
  empty alternative text and do not duplicate announcements.

## Acceptance criteria

- All assets are stored in the repository and load successfully.
- The header lockup is readable without distortion at desktop and mobile sizes.
- The logo and wordmark use a consistent symbol, colours and proportions.
- The annotation overlays the hero without covering its headline or controls.
- Playwright verifies asset loading and captures the resulting header/hero in
  both viewports.

## Scope

Generated raster assets, their integration, provenance and visual checks.
No fictional local photography or printed collateral.

## Design research

Reviewed 12 October 2026. The design applies small-size readability, restrained
lettering and a coherent independent symbol rather than copying another brand.

- [LogoLounge 2026 trends](https://www.logolounge.com/trend/2026-logo-trend-report):
  simple forms and disciplined relationships between marks and wordmarks.
- [Adobe on simple logos](https://www.adobe.com/uk/express/discover/tips/logo/simple):
  clear typography, limited colours and testing at small sizes.
- [Monotype on humanist typography](https://www.monotype.com/resources/how-typography-can-make-your-brand-feel-more-human):
  open, humanist forms as a direction for readable, approachable lettering.
- [Pentagram, Second Home](https://www.pentagram.com/work/second-home):
  an example of approachable type used in a community identity.

Research informs the Woodbrook design; these brands and typefaces are not reused.
