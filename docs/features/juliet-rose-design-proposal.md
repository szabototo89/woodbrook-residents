# Juliet Rose design proposal

Status: Available

## Job to be done

When the Juliet Rose business owner reviews a possible website redesign, they
can explore a realistic responsive concept that communicates Diana's
one-to-one, knowledge-led studio experience and makes the next booking step
clear.

## Visible behaviour

- The homepage leads with the established “Relax and Revitalize” promise and
  immediately locates the studio in Stillorgan.
- Visitors can browse treatment categories and real featured services with
  current durations and prices.
- Studio details, contact information, gift cards, and the booking policy are
  included in the customer journey.
- The layout adapts to desktop and mobile, with an accessible mobile menu and
  booking actions placed at the key decision points shown in the references.
- Tablet screens use a compact landscape hero and two-column treatment grids
  instead of stretching the phone layout.
- The mobile booking action stays out of the way while visitors read down the
  page and returns after the hero when they pause or scroll upward toward a
  decision.
- Brand flourishes and interface icons use transparent raster image assets so
  they remain consistent across browsers without embedded vector markup.
- Booking, gift card, telephone, email, Instagram, and directions actions use
  the business's public destinations and contact details.

## Acceptance criteria

- The design is implemented under `apps/design-proposal`.
- The same presentation-ready proposal is reachable from the Juliet Rose card
  on the main `apps/design-proposals` gallery page.
- The page contains no evaluator notes, placeholder labels, or invented local
  outcomes.
- Real Juliet Rose services, prices, address, opening hours, telephone number,
  and email address are used.
- Laptop, desktop, tablet, and mobile layouts preserve readable content,
  visible calls to action, and usable controls without horizontal page
  overflow.
- The proposal can be built and served independently with Bun workspace
  scripts.

## Scope

This is a presentation-ready static homepage concept. It links to the current
Juliet Rose booking and gift-card flows rather than replacing their commerce
or appointment systems.
